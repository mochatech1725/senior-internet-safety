Deep Fakes and Impersonation

What are deep fakes?

Deep fakes are AI-generated images, videos, or audio that realistically depict people saying or doing things they never did.

Deep fake phishing

What it is

Scammers use AI technology and available audio, video, or images to impersonate people you may know.

The goal

To convince the recipient to take an action

Such as transferring funds, revealing sensitive information, or approving access.

Examples

Deep fake phishing examples

Audio messages

Someone calls you, sounding exactly like one of your family members, and asks for money urgently.

Video calls

Someone emails you asking to set up a meeting, which is then followed up by a real-time AI voice clone or video deepfake call.
